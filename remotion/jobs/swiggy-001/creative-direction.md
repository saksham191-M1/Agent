# Swiggy creative direction

## Concepts considered

1. **One pulse, every plan** — one Swiggy-orange pulse begins with the first tap and
   match-cuts through food, essentials, dining, network proof, and the CTA.
2. **The day unfolds in one flow** — a single vertical route transforms through the
   moments that make up an everyday plan.
3. **Chaos collapses to one screen** — fragmented tabs and decisions compress into one
   focused app surface.

## Selected concept

**One pulse, every plan** is the boldest truthful idea. It gives the film a single visual
 protagonist, creates a strong first-second hook, and makes the match-cut chain legible:

```text
tap ripple -> pulse orb -> food card -> phone -> dining split -> list stack -> stat ring -> CTA
```

## Motion language

- Kinetic type slam in the opening promise.
- Spring-driven pulse rings and scale ramps.
- Push-through browser movement with cursor choreography.
- Device orbit/parallax in the essentials scene.
- Match-cut-ready card stacks and list fanning.
- Split-depth dining scene with a side proof panel.
- Animated counters and a progress bar for verified proof.
- Riser, click, tick, and logo-sting cues aligned to the visual events.

## Director iteration plan

The migrated fixture has been rendered at middle frames for all seven scenes. The next visual
pass should render start, middle, and end frames, then focus upgrades on the opening orb, the
dining split, and the CTA hold before final delivery.
